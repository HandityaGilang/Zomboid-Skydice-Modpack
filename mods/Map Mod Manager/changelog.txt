v1.0 05 Nov 2024
- Uploaded mod to steam workshop.

v1.1 05 Nov 2024
- Updated to make the cell grid and menu button dynamic to your screen resolution.
- Added a discard changes button.

v1.2 08 Nov 2024
- Reduced the width of the scroll lists, giving more space for the grid when you're using a lower resolution.
- Adjusted the close and discard buttons to be dynamic with your screen width.
- Adjusted the height of the Subscribed Map Mods and Dependency lists to be dynamic with your screen height.
- Adjusted several maps having double-up cells in their code, so they appears to conflict with themselves.

v1.3 11 Nov 2024
- Added an Increase Grid button to temporarily hide the scroll lists and increase the size of the grid to enable functionality for lower resolutions.
- Further reduced the width of the scroll lists and padding to allow more space for the grid on lower resolutions.

v1.4 25 Nov 2024
- Added a tooltip to show what maps require the dependency.
- Fixed dependencies incorrectly showing for some maps.

v1.5 25 Dec 2024
- Added localization support.
- Added Polish language support thanks to MrNicDragon and Nacho Juarez.
- Adjusted colours of the dependency scroll list (green for enabled, white for not enabled but subscribed to, red for not subscribed).
- Adjusted dependency tooltip to show further information (dependency required by active map, selected and subscribed map, selected and not subscribed map).
- Added a Select All Maps button to select every single map in the database.
- Fixed Constown variables enabling multiple at a time.
