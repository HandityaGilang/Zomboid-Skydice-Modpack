B42.20 MP Playback Sync Update
2026-08-03

NEW: ADMIN "BAN MUSIC LIST"
- Admins / debug users get a new "Ban Music List" option when right-clicking the GROUND (not a device). It opens a window listing every music item from every loaded music addon, grouped per addon, split into a PLAYABLE list and a BANNED list.
- Drag a song to the other list (or double-click it) to move it; songs always re-file under their own addon category on either side. Press SET (with a confirmation warning) to apply server-wide.
- Addon category headers are collapsible: click a header ([-]/[+]) to fold or unfold its song list - no more doom-scrolling through huge packs. Headers show the song count, and moving a song auto-expands its destination category.
- Banned songs cannot be inserted or played on ANY device (boombox, walkman, portable CD player, HiFi cassette/CD/vinyl decks, vehicle HiFi) - a red halo message tells the player the song was banned. Stop always still works; already-playing songs are not force-stopped.
- Jukeboxes filter banned songs out of their playlist and refuse banned media in their container (this applies to everyone, admins included, since the jukebox validates server-side).
- "Admins/debug can still play banned songs" toggle inside the window controls whether staff bypass the ban on regular devices. Dragging a song back to PLAYABLE and pressing SET unbans it.
- The list is server-authoritative global ModData: it syncs to every client, persists in the save, and the server re-checks the sender's admin access before accepting changes.

PER-DEVICE CLIENT VOLUME + BOOMBOX STATE FIXES
- NEW SANDBOX OPTION "MP Synchronized-Start Handshake" (PZ TrueMoozic page, DEFAULT OFF): controls the multiplayer ready-check that makes every nearby player start a song at the same moment. OFF (default) = pressing Play starts the music almost immediately, no "waiting for other players" cue, and listeners simply join the track mid-play (server-aged timestamps keep them in position). ON = the previous behavior: short ready-check round trip, everyone starts together, up to ~2 seconds delay before the song begins. Applies to boomboxes, placed devices and vehicle radios/HiFis; singleplayer is unaffected either way.
- VOLUME CONTROL REWORK (boombox / walkman / placed vinyl player): the volume slider no longer snaps back to its old position seconds after moving it. The slider was writing the volume onto the ITEM, and the same multiplayer item-echo that used to revert the Play button also reverted the volume. The live playback session (the Tal-style state that already owns Play/Stop) now carries the volume too - the slider reads and drives the session directly, the new value is applied to the running song instantly, and the item copy is synced to the server once so the change also persists. Other players follow the shared now-playing record's volume as before.
- Fixed the PLACED device volume slider (vinyl player, placed boombox) feeling dead: the song actually plays on a world emitter owned by the audio engine, and the slider was retuning the wrong emitter - the change only landed on the next engine cycle (up to ~3 seconds later). Volume and client-volume changes now retune the live world emitter immediately.
- Fixed CLIENT VOLUME being ~2.5x TOO LOUD on placed devices (vinyl player, placed boombox, world fallback sources): the local override was substituted at the wrong scale, skipping the loudness factor every shared volume goes through. The same slider position now sounds the same in local and shared mode.
- The client-volume checkbox now snapshots the CURRENT live volume when ticked (previously it could grab a stale value the server had rolled back), and unticking/toggling retunes the running song immediately on every device type - including placed ones.
- WALKMAN REWORK v2 (session-based): the walkman's play state now lives in a client-local SESSION - the cassette item's data is never written when you press Play or Stop, so the multiplayer item-echo that kept rolling the state back has NOTHING to roll back. This kills the "Play button reverts, song keeps going, Stop and volume stop responding" bug at the root (v1 tried to heal the rolled-back state after the fact; the echo kept winning). Play starts the song instantly - no "waiting for players" handshake, no shared playback record (a walkman is headphones-only; nobody else can ever hear it). Stop always works: the button offers Stop while the session runs even if the server scrambles the item's data. The master/device volume slider is THE walkman volume (the "client volume" checkbox is hidden for walkmans), and volume changes apply to the live song immediately. Power off, battery pull, headphone pull and eject all end the session cleanly. 
- BOOMBOX REWORK session-based, same  the in-hand/on-back boombox now also keeps its play state in a client-local SESSION - Play/Stop never writes the boombox item's data, so the server item-echo can't flip the button back or kill the song anymore. Other players still hear you exactly as before: the shared "now playing" record (which is world data, not item data - the echo can't touch it) carries the song, volume and headphone state, and the synchronized "everyone starts together" handshake is kept. The Stop button shows immediately after pressing Play, stopping during the countdown cancels the start cleanly, and the sync-start can no longer be silently cancelled by an item echo landing during the countdown (a cause of "pressed Play, nothing ever played"). Song end, dropping/unequipping the device, power off and battery pull all retire the session AND the shared record so other players stop hearing you right away; if the server loses the record mid-song it is re-announced automatically.
- REDESIGNED the "client volume" checkbox: it is now PER DEVICE. Ticking it on one boombox no longer hijacks the volume slider of EVERY device (which also made the master/device volume slider appear "dead" everywhere, including placed boomboxes and mute/0). With the box unticked the slider always controls the real shared device volume; ticked, it adjusts your own local listening volume for THAT device only. Applies to boombox/walkman windows, placed devices, HiFi, vehicle HiFi and the jukebox. Old saved global setting is discarded on first load.
- Placed devices: volume changes now reach OTHER clients reliably - remote listeners follow the transmitted now_play volume first instead of a possibly stale local device copy.
- The Stop button now shows immediately after pressing Play (during the synchronized-start countdown) instead of showing "Play" for ~2 seconds - which invited double-clicks that desynced the state.
- Pressing Stop during the synchronized-start countdown now cancels the scheduled start (previously the song could start anyway with the UI already showing stopped).
- Fixed eject sometimes EATING tapes from third-party music packs (SpegHeddy etc.) and tapes inserted on older saves: the server give now resolves short item names across ALL loaded modules instead of only the base Tsarcraft pack.

MULTIPLAYER PLAYBACK SYNC OVERHAUL (BOOMBOX / WALKMAN)
- Fixed the Play/Stop button flipping back by itself a few seconds after pressing it. The server was TOGGLING its copy of the device instead of setting it, so once client and server disagreed they flipped in opposite directions forever.
- Fixed music continuing to play after the tape/CD was taken out of the device. The server was broadcasting its own unstoppable copy of the song to every nearby player.
- Fixed songs not being heard by other players and the general "everyone hears something different" desync. Playback state changes now push the device's real state to the server immediately, and the server stamps that state onto its copy of the item (absolute, never toggled).
- Fixed a server-side crash when turning off / removing the battery or headphones from a playing device - the crash silently aborted the operation on the server, leaving the device permanently desynced.
- Fixed the "ItemStats" packet error spam (Cannot invoke getItemContainer... containingItem is null) that was crashing item sync for everyone nearby. Removing a tape no longer creates a client-only ghost copy, and items inside bags/CD cases are no longer synced in a way other clients can't resolve.
- Fixed "remove tape to fanny pack / satchel / glove box" doing nothing in multiplayer.
- Fixed a device staying stuck on "Stop" (still marked playing) after a song ended naturally.
- Devices now self-heal: if your walkman/boombox audio and its state ever fall out of step (lag, packet loss), it is reconciled within a few seconds - ghost audio is stopped, and if the server loses track of your song mid-play it is re-announced so other players keep hearing it.

NEW: SYNCHRONIZED START ("HEY EVERYONE, WE'RE PLAYING THIS NOW")
- Pressing Play on a boombox, walkman, placed device or car radio now announces the song to EVERY client on the server first. The server runs a ready-check: "HEY, WE'RE PLAYING THIS" -> every client answers "ok, ready" -> the server calls "everyone's ready - START" and all clients (including the player who pressed Play) begin the track together, within each other's latency.
- One lagging player can never hold the music hostage: if someone doesn't answer within 1.5 seconds the server starts the song anyway, and every client keeps its own fallback timer in case the start signal is lost.
- Every client runs the song locally no matter how far away they are: too far to hear means zero volume, NOT "not playing". Walk toward a device and the music fades in already at the correct position in the song - and the sound's world position is tracked the whole time, so it fades in from exactly where the device is (and follows it if it moves).
- Fade in/out by distance and the personal "Client volume" setting are unchanged.
- Applies to boomboxes, walkmans, placed/world devices (including ones wired to speakers) and vehicle radios/HiFis.

FIXES
- Fixed the jukebox (and placed HiFi tape/CD/vinyl, and car radios) STOPPING or skipping tracks for EVERYONE when one listener walked far away. The audio engine silently kills a far player's local copy of the sound, and the mod misread that as "the track ended" - that far player then stopped/advanced the shared device state for the whole server. Distant clients now suspend their local audio instead and re-attach as they come back, never touching the shared state.
- Music sound range raised to 500 tiles (was 120; base mod originally 50, bundled packs 75) so the engine no longer kills a song's silent far copy anywhere inside the loaded world. This is what makes "walk toward a device and the music fades in mid-song" work reliably - the practical limit is now world streaming, not the audio engine. The audible fade is unchanged (full volume to 80 tiles, silent at 100): vehicle radios and vehicle HiFis previously leaned on the engine's own distance rolloff for this, so they now use the mod's manual 80-to-100-tile fade like every other device.
- Vehicle HiFi audio management range raised from 30 to 110 tiles - beyond 30 tiles the CD/cassette sound used to be torn down entirely and restarted from 0:00 on approach.
- Fixed walking up to a device that had been playing for a while and hearing nothing until it changed tracks (dead local sound handle after an engine cull is now detected and restarted on approach).
- The HiFi stereo installed in a vehicle now gets the cassette "Music options" icon in the vehicle radial menu (like the Valu-Tech car radio) and opens the HiFi window directly.
- Fixed placed boomboxes losing their "Device options" right-click menu on dedicated servers. The placed device was linked to its inventory item by an ID the server reassigns, so the link went stale; the device now re-links itself automatically whenever the stamped ID no longer matches.
- Fixed a placed boombox EATING the inserted tape/CD when picked up on a dedicated server (same stale link: the pickup found no linked device state and wiped the item's own media record). Pickup now merges device state instead of overwriting, so the media always survives.
- Fixed the server log spam "ObjectModDataPacket: object is null" when placing a boombox: the device's data was pushed to the server before the server knew the object existed, and the packet (with the device-item link in it) was silently dropped. The push is now delayed until the placement has registered.
- Fixed a placed boombox FORGETTING EVERYTHING (cassette gone, power off) after walking away and coming back on a dedicated server, while the music kept blaring with no way to stop it. Root cause: the server never stored ANY of the device's state - insert, play, volume and power lived only on the acting player's client, so the device rebuilt factory-empty from the server's copy while the "someone is playing music" record stayed alive under a key nobody could reach anymore. Every insert/eject/play/stop/volume/power action is now stamped by the SERVER onto the device item on the floor (the one thing it actually persists), the rebuilt device restores cassette + power + volume from it, and stop/power-off/eject now kill the shared music record by the device's POSITION - stale record keys can no longer make a song unstoppable.
- Fixed the placed-device POWER switch never being sent to the server at all (it only ever flipped on the acting player's own screen).
- Fixed all the "require failed" WARN spam at startup (ISItemDropBox / ISVolumeBar / ISHotbar / ISWaitAction) - those vanilla classes are already loaded before any mod file runs; the failing lookups were pure log noise.

FULL DEVICE SWEEP (MP PERSISTENCE FOR EVERYTHING ELSE)
- Audited EVERY device type in the mod for the same "server never saw it" class of bug. Portable boombox/walkman, vehicle radios and HiFis, jukeboxes, speakers, vinyl albums, CD cases and CD stomping were already routed correctly. Two device families were not:
- Placed HiFi stereos (CD deck, cassette deck, vinyl turntable): NONE of the deck state ever reached the server - the placed HiFi is a client-local object, so its raw state pushes were silently dropped. Inserting media, ejecting, play/stop, next/prev track and the auto-stop on power loss now go through a server command that stamps the deck state onto the HiFi item on the floor (the thing the server actually persists). Walk away and come back, relog, or have another player use it - the decks keep their media, current track and state.
- Placed HiFi media was also EATEN on pickup and on device rebuild (only the boombox cassette record was carried over, never the HiFi decks). The CD/cassette/vinyl in the decks now ride along on pickup and are restored when the placed HiFi rebuilds.
- Ejecting a CD/cassette/vinyl from a placed HiFi created the item CLIENT-SIDE ONLY - a ghost copy the server never knew about (can't be equipped, can't be dropped, ItemStats errors for everyone nearby). Ejects are now created by the server; inserts now tell the server the media left your inventory (no re-appearing media on relog).
- CD scratch/wear now survives the HiFi CD deck and the portable CD player eject (it was being wiped by the server-side re-give).
- Portable CD player (SWTC): every state change (insert, eject, play/stop) now syncs the item to the server. Before, the server kept a stale copy of the player - B42's item transaction check then silently refused to equip or drop it, and its state died on relog.

JUKEBOX-STYLE LIVE-STATE RECONCILE (BOOMBOX / PLACED DEVICES)
- The jukebox never desyncs because it derives its state from the shared "someone is playing music" table that every client receives - so the placed boombox now does the same. When a placed device is rebuilt (walk away and come back, relog), it adopts the live playback record for its tile: if a song is playing there, the device now correctly shows POWER ON and the STOP button (instead of "off + Play" while the tile keeps blaring), and pressing Stop actually kills the song.
- Pressing Play on a placed device now also stamps "on + playing + cassette + volume" onto the device item on the floor locally (Stop stamps "not playing"), so the acting player's own copy stays coherent even between server round-trips.
- Fixed ejecting a cassette from a placed boombox (and walkman/boombox in hand, in multiplayer) giving NOTHING back. The eject stopped creating the tape client-side (that was the ghost-item fix), but the server-side "give the tape back" was only sent when a client-side copy was found - which no longer ever existed. The give is now unconditional.

VEHICLE HIFI (CASSETTE + CD STEREO) FIXES
- The cassette "Music options" icon is the ONLY way to open the mod's vehicle radio/HiFi window. The vanilla "Device options" radial slice and the dashboard radio icon are left completely untouched and always open the BASE-GAME radio UI - for True MOOZIC devices too - so other mods and the vanilla flow keep working exactly as before.
- Fixed the vehicle HiFi's music EMITTER LAGGING BEHIND a moving car, which made the audio bounce between the left and right headphone channels. Two causes: the emitter position was only updated every 20 ticks (it now follows the vehicle every tick), and the sound stayed 3D-positional for the driver - it now switches to flat 2D while you're inside the vehicle (the same thing the Valu-Tech / Premium Technologies radios already did, which is why they never bounced).
- Fixed the vehicle HiFi EATING its cassette/CD when you exit and re-enter the vehicle (media visible for a split second in the radial/device window, then it deletes itself and the music stops). Inserting or ejecting media never told the SERVER about the vehicle part's new state - only pressing Play/Stop did - so the server's copy of the HiFi had empty decks, and re-entering the vehicle resynced the part from the server, wiping the media. Insert and eject now transmit the part state immediately (the same call the play buttons already used). This also means the media finally survives for OTHER players and across relogs.
- Fixed ejecting a tape/CD from a CAR RADIO eating it in multiplayer (or the tape flashing into the inventory and being deleted a moment later). Two causes: the car eject actually runs a "remove to glovebox" action variant that the multiplayer give-the-tape-back hook never recognized (only the plain eject was covered - the bag/glovebox variants relied on a dead code path that stopped existing after build 41); and the hook still contained a leftover "remove the client-side ghost copy" sweep from the old design that could only ever delete a REAL tape now - including the one the server had just handed back. All eject variants (plain, glovebox, fanny pack, satchel, backpack) now go through the server-side give, and the ghost sweep is gone.

SERVER-SIDE PROTECTION (MP HARDENING)
- The server no longer blindly trusts playback commands from clients. Automatic "the song stopped" reports now carry a token identifying WHICH playback session they observed ending; if the device has since started a newer song, the stale report is rejected instead of killing the new track for everyone. Deliberate user actions (pressing Stop, ejecting media) are always honored.
- Only players close enough to actually hear a device can affect its playback: stop, media, volume and world-object commands from a sender far beyond earshot are dropped by the server. Even a client running broken or outdated mod files can no longer corrupt shared music state for the whole server.
- Server boot cleanup: leftover "someone is playing music" records from before a restart are cleared at startup. These are keyed by connection IDs that get reassigned after a restart, so a stale record could glue music to the wrong player. Placed/world device records are kept (their keys are stable) so jukebox-style persistence still works.
- Clients now detect a server restart / live lua reload (per-boot token) and flush all locally running mod audio, so nothing keeps ghost-playing from the previous session.

NEW: DEVICE STATUS TEXT (vanilla TV/radio style)
- Short status lines now appear above the player using a device - or above the placed device itself - just like base-game TVs and radios.
- Pressing Play in multiplayer walks you through the sync: "Sending MP handshake..." -> "Waiting for players..." -> "Playing <song>". While the ready-check runs, a single CD-seek noise plays as a wait cue and is cut off the instant the track starts.
- Stopping shows "Sending stop to server..." then "Stopped - synced" (just "Stopped" in singleplayer).
- Inserting and ejecting media, skipping to the next/previous track, and toggling power ("Power ON"/"Power OFF") all show their own line.
- The jukebox announces every song of a looping playlist above the cabinet as it starts; placed devices announce their song above the device for everyone nearby.
- The text only ever appears above the acting player or the device - never over other players' heads.
- MULTIPLAYER: other players nearby now SEE the status lines too. The action is relayed through the server (with the same distance limit as hearing the device) and every player reads it in their OWN language.
- All status lines translated into all 27 supported languages.

B42.20 MP Update
2026-08-01

MULTIPLAYER FIXES
- Fixed the anti-cheat kick ("SyncItemDelete: no capability") when inserting headphones, batteries, cassettes, vinyl or CDs into devices on dedicated servers. All item add/remove operations are now server-authoritative.
- Fixed boomboxes (and other held/placed players) being silent to OTHER players in multiplayer. Playback state is now published through the server instead of each client overwriting the shared music table - car radios, boomboxes, walkmans and placed devices are all heard correctly by everyone nearby.
- Fixed music randomly stopping or becoming uncontrollable after moving a device between inventory, hands and the ground. Each transition now cleanly hands the sound over to the new location.

PICKUP OVERHAUL
- Picking up a placed, playing boombox no longer eats the tape.
- A playing boombox now goes straight to your primary hand when grabbed and KEEPS PLAYING - the music follows you instead of dying or staying behind on the ground.
- Battery charge, headphones and inserted media all survive the ground <-> inventory round trip in MP.

LOOT & DISTRIBUTIONS
- Fixed CD albums from add-on CD packs never spawning in loot. TrueMoozic now injects detected CD packs into world distributions with their own "CD Spawn Rate (%)" sandbox option.
- New sandbox option "Max Music Media Per Container" (default 5): caps how many cassettes, vinyls and CDs (each type separately) can roll in a single container so music doesn't crowd out other loot. Set 0 for no cap.
- New sandbox option "Generate New Loot Mid-Save" (default off): add a new music pack to an existing save and its media will be sprinkled into already-looted containers as chunks load.

VEHICLES
- Fixed the "Spawn TrueMoozic StepVan" sandbox option not working on dedicated servers, and its default is now OFF.

NEW FEATURE: CLIENT-SIDE VOLUME
- New "Client volume" checkbox next to the volume slider in every player window (boombox, CD player, HiFi).
- When checked, the slider controls how loud YOU hear all TrueMoozic music - without changing the device volume other players hear. Your setting is remembered between sessions. Off by default.

NEW FEATURE: SCRATCHED CDs
- Loose CDs (found outside a CD case) can now spawn scratched. Scratched CDs get a "- X% scratched" label on their name.
- 25% scratched: every 2nd song skips ahead after a few seconds.
- 50% scratched: every song skips except one lucky track.
- 100% scratched: every song skips almost immediately, and the disc stops after the last track instead of looping.
- CDs found inside CD cases are always clean.
- New sandbox option "CD Scratches" (default 75): the chance a loose CD spawns clean. Set 100 to disable scratches entirely.

TRANSLATIONS
- All new sandbox options and UI text translated into all 27 supported languages.
